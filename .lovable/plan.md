# Four-slide creative editable PowerPoint sample

## Goal
Create a standalone four-slide PowerPoint concept deck that demonstrates a much bolder, less repetitive design direction before applying it to the full 97-slide presentation.

## Slides
- Executive thesis: turn one existing strategy slide into a high-impact editorial composition with oversized type and a strong focal statement.
- Platform architecture: express the shared platform as a layered visual system rather than a card grid.
- Strategic roadmap: show progression as a continuous horizon field with milestones, movement, and clear future-state emphasis.
- Executive decision: transform an existing decision slide into a decisive board-level composition with asymmetric hierarchy and a strong closing statement.

## Design direction
- Keep Schneider green, white, graphite, and restrained lime/amber accents.
- Use large typographic scale, asymmetric grids, precision rules, negative space, and distinct visual silhouettes across all four slides.
- Preserve native editable text, shapes, lines, and diagrams in PowerPoint.
- Use only existing deck content, without inventing claims or metrics.

## Validation
- Generate one standalone 16:9 editable `.pptx` with exactly four slides.
- Validate the PowerPoint package, render every slide to images, inspect all four, then complete at least one refinement and re-verification pass.
