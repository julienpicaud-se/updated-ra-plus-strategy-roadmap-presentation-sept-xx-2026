# Expand Roadmap Foundations

## Goal
Grow the first section from 9 to 16 slides, keeping every current slide and adding a second layer of practical executive guidance. Reuse the strongest previously drafted concepts where repetition reinforces the message, and add new pages that connect roadmap theory to leadership action.

## Additions
1. **Why roadmaps matter** after the opening: align investment, focus teams, communicate direction and expose trade-offs.
2. **From strategy to outcomes** after the definition: show the chain from company goal to product outcome, opportunity, bet and evidence.
3. **Roadmap maturity ladder** after the connected-layers slide: move from feature list to strategic, evidence-led portfolio.
4. **Confidence by horizon** after the quarter-versus-commitment slide: use Now, Next and Later to explain decreasing precision.
5. **Customer-facing language** after delivery certainty: define safe wording for directional, planned, forecast and committed work.
6. **Roadmap review cadence** after the portfolio example: separate monthly health checks from quarterly portfolio decisions.
7. **Executive conversation guide** before the final decisions slide: provide the questions leaders should ask without turning the review into a feature debate.

## Presentation approach
- Use the existing columns and table layouts so every page remains visually consistent and export-safe.
- Keep text concise and executive-ready, with no new unverified figures, dates or customer claims.
- Preserve the current section sequence and allow intentional overlap between roadmap definition, maturity and certainty pages.
- Update section boundaries dynamically so section downloads and the full PowerPoint include all added pages.

## Validation
- Render all expanded foundation pages on desktop and mobile without clipping.
- Download the expanded first section and confirm its page count and file integrity.
- Download the complete deck and confirm the total page count, section coverage and file integrity.
