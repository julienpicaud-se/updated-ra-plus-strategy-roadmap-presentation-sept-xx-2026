# Refine the editable PowerPoint design

## Goal
Make the editable PowerPoint feel more polished, premium, and executive-ready while keeping every slide native, editable, and one-to-one with the 97 live screens.

## Design improvements
- Strengthen the shared slide frame with more deliberate title spacing, a refined eyebrow treatment, and a subtle, consistent page signature.
- Upgrade cards and tables with cleaner surface contrast, lighter borders, stronger internal hierarchy, and restrained green accents.
- Improve title and section slides with more confident scale, asymmetry, and visual depth while preserving Schneider’s green identity.
- Refine roadmap boards, timelines, statistics, and strategic diagrams so labels, milestones, and relationships scan faster.
- Harmonize the specialist slide types so the full deck feels authored as one presentation rather than assembled from separate templates.
- Keep all content, slide order, section boundaries, and the pixel-faithful image export unchanged.

## Validation
- Generate the complete editable deck and confirm it remains exactly 97 slides.
- Validate and reopen the PowerPoint package through an independent Office renderer.
- Render representative slides from every major slide type and inspect typography, spacing, clipping, contrast, and alignment.
- Complete at least one visual fix-and-verify cycle before delivery.

## Technical details
- Apply improvements through reusable PptxGenJS helpers and slide-type renderers, not flattened images.
- Preserve the established Windows-compatible export pattern: no custom slide master, no unused notes parts, and corrected image metadata.
- Keep Inter typography with the existing safe PowerPoint fallback behavior.
