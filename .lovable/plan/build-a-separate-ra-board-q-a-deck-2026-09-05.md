# Build a separate RA+ Board Q&A deck

## Deliverable
- Create a separate, editable PowerPoint deck, not additional slides in the main roadmap.
- Add a dedicated **Board Q&A** export alongside the existing roadmap export.
- Keep the deck concise and designed for live board discussion.

## Storyline
1. Cover and executive answer: why RA+ wins and what the board is being asked to believe.
2. Roadmap Q&A: prioritisation, timing confidence, dependencies, delivery capacity, 2027 outcomes, and the directional post-2027 horizon.
3. Competitor Q&A: where RA+ wins, where competitors are stronger, price evidence limits, AI maturity, and the proof required to sustain differentiation.
4. Value case Q&A: growth and retention, cost to serve, risk and capital, investment gates, measurement ownership, and the figures Finance still needs to validate.
5. Closing board checklist: decisions, evidence, owners, and next proof points.

## Design and evidence discipline
- Use Calibri and the established Schneider Electric dark-green executive identity.
- Use large question headlines, short direct answers, and evidence or implication callouts rather than dense bullet pages.
- Do not invent competitor pricing, market claims, roadmap commitments, or financial figures.
- Mark post-2027 milestones as directional and unknown value figures as validation requirements.

## Validation
- Type-check the implementation.
- Export and structurally validate the separate PowerPoint.
- Extract its text to confirm content and order.
- Render and visually inspect every slide, fixing any clipping, overlap, density, or contrast issues before delivery.
